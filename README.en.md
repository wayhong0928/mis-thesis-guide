# Research Methods and Thesis Writing Knowledge Network

A research methods knowledge base for social science and information management (IM) research. It turns study notes, methodology books, public guidelines, and hands-on practice with AI-assisted research into a static website and a set of Markdown files you can keep coming back to.

**Website**: <https://wayhong0928.github.io/mis-thesis-guide/>

**The site is in Traditional Chinese.** This page is the only English part.

The site is a personal set of study notes. AI tools helped gather material and draft text; the author reviewed the content. It is not an official standard: your department's rules and your advisor come first. Go back to the original sources when citing.

## Intended readers

The default setting is **information management research with a social science orientation**, and most readers are graduate students. Statistical criteria, chapter conventions, and writing rules are field-specific; readers in other fields should follow their own department's rules.

## What it covers

A collection of personal study notes in six sections:

| Section | Contents |
|---|---|
| Research foundations | The first month from scratch, what research is and how long it takes, finding and reading papers and choosing a topic, journal and conference lists, the scope of IM research, research questions and hypotheses, literature reviews and critical thinking, how theories are built and evaluated, common theory families |
| Research methods | A map for choosing methods, research ethics and data management, systematic and scoping reviews, planning quantitative analysis and data handling, surveys, experiments, system development and design science research (DSR), algorithms and data analysis, secondary data and archival research, qualitative research |
| Thesis writing | Thesis structure and chapter-by-chapter guidance, discipline in academic Chinese writing, proposals and defense slides, revision, archiving, and wrap-up after the defense |
| AI-assisted research | Workflows and a three-layer structure, the AI tool landscape and its risks, academic ethics and AI disclosure; the methodological judgment calls and red lines live here |
| AI tools and environment | How to set up and choose AI tools lives in [ai-agent-notes](https://wayhong0928.github.io/ai-agent-notes/); this site keeps only pointer pages |
| Toolbox | Reference management and knowledge bases, a guide to AI research tools, checklists, AI prompt templates, a glossary and usage table, further reading, about the site |

## What it is not

- **Not an official standard** (see the note at the top).
- **No topics, frameworks, or constructs from any specific study.** All writing examples are generic, textbook-level ones.
- **No summaries of other people's theses or reports**, and **no reproduction of reference books or lectures**: only bibliographic entries, links, and short reviews. See `docs/about.md`.

## Layout

```
docs/      Content source (Markdown, readable directly in Obsidian)
pages/     HTML generated by build.py; don't edit by hand
assets/    Shared styles and scripts
build.py   Generates pages/ and index.html from docs/
```

To edit content, add pages, or host your own copy, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) (Chinese).

## Reporting problems and updates

If something is wrong, open an [issue](https://github.com/wayhong0928/mis-thesis-guide/issues) and the author will correct or remove it. Regulations and journal policies are rechecked once a semester (last round: 2026-09). What changed on each page is in the changelog on [About this site](https://wayhong0928.github.io/mis-thesis-guide/pages/about.html).
